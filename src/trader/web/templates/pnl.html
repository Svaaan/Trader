What it would have done

A paper account, filled at the first open after each signal and closed at that session's close. No orders are placed anywhere and no broker is involved — this is a record, not a trade.

This is the only measurement here that cannot be mined: when each line was written the outcome had not happened yet. That is worth more than any backtest, and it is fragile in exactly one way — so the model never reads this ledger. It is a scorecard, never an input. The moment a system tunes itself on its own paper results, they stop being a forward test and become another training set.
